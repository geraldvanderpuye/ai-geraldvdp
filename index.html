The 60-second AI test

Is AI changing your business — or just helping people in it?

Seven questions. No jargon. The test scores the whole business, not your keenest employee — individuals experiment, the organisation adopts, workflows transform, the business changes.

  • Individual experimentation — individuals use AI, but the business has not adopted it.
  • Organisational adoption — AI use has breadth, intention and repetition.
  • Workflow transformation — AI completes defined work and the business measures the outcome.
  • Business reinvention — AI changes the proposition, customer value, delivery model or economics.

Four levels. Most businesses are still at the first. Ask me where yours sits →
