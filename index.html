The work behind the point of view

I connect what AI makes possible with what the market needs.

People often arrive asking which AI tool they should use. The better question usually appears later: what could this let the business do that it cannot do today?

For the first time, small teams can build companies worth hundreds of millions. That changes what a business should even attempt.

My work brings three questions together: what can AI make possible, what do customers actually need, and how does the business take it to market and grow?

That is what I work on in the Think AI-Native series — live, streamed online and inside companies. Right now that means helping companies like Heineken and real-estate firms think AI-natively.

Experience across Rackspace, GCP and machine learning. Workshops and programmes delivered for Brixton Brewery, part of Heineken · Levy Real Estate · Lambeth Council-funded programmes · Brixton BID · small businesses · community AI events in partnership with Stripe.

Intelligence has been democratized. This is the moment we’ll all remember — the question is what you did with it.
